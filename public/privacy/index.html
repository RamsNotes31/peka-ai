Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Peka AI ("Peka AI", "we", "us") operates the website peka.ai.id and the Peka AI content publishing application (the "App"). The App is used by Peka AI to publish its own editorial content to Peka AI's own social media accounts through official platform APIs, including the TikTok Content Posting API. This policy explains what information the App processes and how it is used.

Information we process

  • Connected account information. When an account is connected to the App through a platform's official authorization flow, we receive basic account information made available by that platform, such as the account's user ID, username, display name, and avatar, together with an access token used to act on that account's behalf.
  • Content submitted for publishing. Media files (images and videos), captions, and related metadata that Peka AI prepares for publication to its own accounts.
  • Technical logs. Publishing results returned by platform APIs, such as post identifiers and success or error statuses, kept for operational records.

The App does not collect information from the public, from visitors of peka.ai.id, or from any third-party social media users. It operates only on accounts owned and authorized by Peka AI.

How we use the information

  • To authenticate the connected accounts and publish Peka AI's content to them.
  • To keep an internal record of what was published and when.
  • To diagnose publishing errors and maintain the reliability of the App.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing. We do not use connected account data for advertising or profiling.

Data storage and retention

Access tokens and publishing logs are stored on Peka AI's private servers with access restricted to the account owner. Tokens are kept only while the account remains connected to the App and are deleted when the connection is revoked or the App is decommissioned. A connected account owner can revoke the App's access at any time from the platform's own settings, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

Third-party platforms

The App interacts with third-party platforms (such as TikTok) through their official APIs. Content published through the App is subject to the respective platform's own terms and privacy policy once it is posted there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The current version will always be available at this address, with the revision date shown at the top.
